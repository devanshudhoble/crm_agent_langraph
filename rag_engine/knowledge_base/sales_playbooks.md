# Sales Playbook: Enterprise Technical Stakeholder Meetings (CTO & VP Eng)

## Context & Objectives
Technical buyers (CTOs, VPs of Engineering, CISOs) value operational reliability, security posture, and proactive transparency above commercial pitches. If a prospective client is currently testing in a sandbox or staging environment and experiencing friction, pitching new features without resolving operational bugs will destroy executive trust.

## The 3-Step FDE Executive Alignment Playbook

### Step 1: Open with Proactive Support & Operational Transparency
- **Golden Rule**: Always address open tickets before presenting commercial or roadmap updates.
- If the customer has open high/medium severity tickets (e.g. API latency, SSO renewal), explicitly acknowledge them first:
  > *"Before we discuss expansion, I want to address the two open items in your staging environment—specifically the API timeout webhook and the SSO token issue. Our engineering team has a patch scheduled, and here is our exact resolution timeline."*
- **Outcome**: Converts a potential deal-breaker into proof of white-glove engineering support.

### Step 2: Present Value Proposition & Targeted Product Fit
- Ground the value in the customer's specific evaluation tier (e.g., Enterprise Core Tier at ₹25L).
- Connect their operational priorities (e.g., automation of compliance, data privacy, latency) to our Enterprise AI platform capabilities.
- Address specific concerns raised in prior interactions (e.g., Sept 28 architecture meeting questions on data sovereignty).

### Step 3: Present Case Study X (Peer Validation)
- Walk through **Case Study X** (Top Private Tier-1 Bank).
- Emphasize how an identical architecture handled similar scale while adhering to strict banking regulations.
- Highlight metrics: 92% prep time reduction and seamless RBI audit clearance.

### Step 4: Secure Definitive Next Steps & Contract Timeline
- Confirm staging sandbox acceptance criteria with CTO.
- Lock in contract review timeline targeting Q4 closing.
