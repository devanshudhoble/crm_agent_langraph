# Case Studies: Enterprise AI

## Case Study X: Top Private Tier-1 Bank (BFSI Core Rollout)

### Executive Overview
A leading private sector bank in India with over 15 million digital banking customers deployed our Enterprise AI Platform to automate compliance validation, KYC anomaly resolution, and relationship manager intelligence.

### Key Pain Points Prior to Deployment
1. Relationship managers spent 45 minutes manually preparing customer background notes across CRM, Core Banking, and loan servicing systems before client meetings.
2. Inconsistent follow-ups and unaddressed IT/support tickets frequently threatened multimillion-rupee contract renewals.
3. Strict banking data sovereignty requirements strictly prevented third-party public cloud LLM processing.

### Solution Architecture
- Deployed Enterprise AI platform inside the bank's AWS Mumbai VPC.
- Connected 6 backend systems via Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers (Core Banking, Salesforce CRM, Jira Service Desk, and Email archives).
- Integrated LangGraph state machines for multi-step contextual synthesis and meeting prep generation.

### Business Impact & Results
- **92% reduction in meeting prep time**: Dropped from 45 minutes to under 30 seconds per meeting.
- **₹18 Crore ($2.2M) pipeline acceleration**: Deals progressed 35% faster through contracting stages.
- **Zero regulatory infractions**: Successfully passed RBI third-party software compliance audit with zero findings.
- **100% resolution tracking**: Support ticket roadblocks addressed proactively prior to high-stakes stakeholder renewals.
